## LinkedIn

```
August's jobs number was revised this morning, from +162,000 to +133,000. The bigger story is underneath the headline.

With the full industry detail for August now published, two industries added 159,700 jobs between them. That is more than the entire headline.

Restaurants and other eating places: +110,400
Local government education: +49,300

Both were bouncing back from July. Restaurants lost 94,000 jobs in July and local government education lost 52,000. Over the two months together, restaurants are up 16,400 and local government education is down 2,700.

That changes the takeaway on how strong August was. A month that looked solid at the headline was highly concentrated in two industries, and both were recovering what they had lost a month earlier. Our tool marks the August restaurant move as an anomaly: 4.8 usual swings above a normal month for that industry.

A few takeaways from today's September print:

- The headline was +29,000.
- Health care and social assistance added 23,000, about 79% of the headline.
- Government lost 17,000.
- Temporary help services lost 10,900.
- July was revised again, from +21,000 to -10,000.

The headline alone does not show any of this. In our view, payroll data has to be studied at the industry level to make sense of it.

That is why we built the U.S. Jobs Data Explorer. It is free, it is updated through September, and it marks the moves that are unusual for each industry. Use the tool:

https://www.data4thepeople.com/p/nonfarm-payrolls-by-industry
```
