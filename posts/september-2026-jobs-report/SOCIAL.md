### LinkedIn

```
The jobs report said the U.S. added 29,000 jobs in September. The BLS's own margin of error on that number runs from -93,000 to +151,000.

Over the last 12 months, only 4 monthly estimates had a range that was all gains.

That doesn't make the data useless. It means the headline is the least useful part of it. Look one level down and August's revised single-point estimate, 133,000 jobs, tells a different story: 83,100 of those jobs came from two industries, Food services and drinking places and Local government, education, and those two industries pay about half of what everyone else earns.

Over the past three years, 82% of all jobs added in this country have been in Health care and social assistance.

We walk through how to read the jobs report by industry, revision and pay, with charts:
https://www.data4thepeople.com/p/september-2026-jobs-report
```

## Notes

- **Looping.** Neither site has a loop setting for an uploaded video; each feed
  decides how it plays, and that changes from time to time. Watch the preview
  on each site. If one plays the video once and stops and the loop matters, the
  GIF is the version that always loops.
- **Check the GIF on LinkedIn before posting.** LinkedIn has changed how it
  handles uploaded GIFs more than once; look at the preview to confirm it moves.
  If it shows a still, the alternative is to upload `images/01-margin-of-error.png`.
- **Links.** X shows one link card per post, so each X post carries only the
  article link. The article embeds the video and links to the explorer.
- No hashtags, as in the earlier copy.
