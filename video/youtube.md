## Description

```
A 45-second tour of the U.S. Jobs Data Explorer, a free interactive treemap of nonfarm payroll employment by industry from the monthly BLS jobs report.

Try it here: https://www.data4thepeople.com/p/nonfarm-payrolls-by-industry

In this video:
1. Read a tile: size is jobs gained or lost, blue is a gain, red is a loss
2. Pick any month (here, August 2026 after its revision)
3. Hover for the anomaly score: a hatched tile is unusual for that industry, not just large
4. Click a tile to drill down, and click All industries to go back
5. Compare 1 month to 20 years, in jobs or percent
6. Copy a link to the exact view, or download a CSV or PNG

The headline jobs number is one figure. Underneath it are 842 industry series that BLS publishes, and the industry detail often tells a different story than the headline. The explorer scores every move against that industry's own history, so you can see which changes stand out.

Data: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, Current Employment Statistics (CES), seasonally adjusted. Broad totals go back to 1939; most industry detail starts in 1990. Updated after every jobs report.

Built by Data 4 The People. Free to use, no signup.
https://www.data4thepeople.com
```
